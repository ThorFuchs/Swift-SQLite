# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on the work.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in
issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/ThorFuchs). Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove content, and warn, suspend or ban participants who break
these rules.
